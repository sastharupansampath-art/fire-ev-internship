## Day 15 — CMOS Inverter Schematic
Today, I studied the CMOS inverter circuit and prepared its simulation netlist. I connected a PMOS transistor to VDD and an NMOS transistor to ground, with their gates connected to the input and drains connected to the output. I set up a DC sweep to study the voltage transfer characteristic (VTC).

## Day 16 — Dynamic Behaviour
Today, I studied the transient response of a CMOS inverter. I used a pulse input and a load capacitor to observe how the output changes over time. I reviewed rise time, fall time, propagation delay and the effect of load capacitance.

## Day 17 — CMOS Power Consumption
Today, I studied static power, dynamic power and short-circuit power in CMOS circuits. I reviewed the dynamic power equation and understood how supply voltage, load capacitance and switching frequency affect power consumption.

## Day 18 — Inverter Sizing
Today, I studied CMOS inverter sizing by changing the PMOS width while keeping the NMOS dimensions and other simulation conditions fixed. I prepared a comparison to observe how transistor sizing affects switching behaviour, rise time and fall time.
